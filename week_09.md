## Content Insights
- I initially thought that .scss and .sass files were the same, and learned that there are syntax differences. Sass being more concise since curly braces `{}` and semicolons `;` were omitted, and SCSS or Sassy CSS retaining the traditional CSS syntax.
- Another insight was that hyphens `-` and underscores `_` were treated similarly in Sass, removing a formatting concern during development.
