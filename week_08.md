# Learning Experiment: The Effect of Whole-Topic vs. Chunked Learning on Retention

## Experiment Setup
### Resources:
[Chunking and sequencing learning - nsw.gov.au](https://education.nsw.gov.au/teaching-and-learning/curriculum/explicit-teaching/explicit-teaching-strategies/chunking-and-sequencing-learning#What0)  
[Chunking(psychology) - Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chunking_(psychology))

**Topics Studied:**  
- **Whole-Topic Approach:** PHP Arrays  
- **Chunking Approach:** PHP Functions  

**Hypothesis:**  
Breaking a topic into smaller chunks with self-assessments after each section leads to better retention and understanding than studying a topic as a whole with one self-assessment at the end.

**Method:**  
1. **Whole-Topic Approach:**  
   - Studied PHP Arrays as one cohesive topic for 45 minutes in a single session.  
   - Completed one self-assessment at the end of the session to evaluate.
2. **Chunking Approach:**  
   - Divided PHP Functions into two sections:  
     1. Basic syntax and structure.  
     2. Practical use cases with examples.  
   - Studied each section in sequence, conducting a self-assessment after each section to solidify understanding.
3. **Retention Test:**  
   - After 24 hours, tested recall and application for both topics by solving relevant tasks and answering concept-based questions.


## Results
### Whole-Topic Approach (PHP Arrays):
- **Immediate Self-Assessment Score:** 65%  
- **Retention Score (24 hours later):** Recall was average for basic concepts but slightly lower for more detailed topics like multidimensional arrays.  
- **Learning Experience:** The session felt overwhelming, as there was no opportunity to review individual concepts during study.

### Chunking Approach (PHP Functions):
- **Self-Assessments:**  
  - Section 1: 70%  
  - Section 2: 75%  
- **Retention Score (24 hours later):** Recall was stronger and more detailed for both syntax and practical examples.  
- **Learning Experience:** Felt more structured and manageable, with clear progress after each self-assessment.


## Learning Insights

- The chunking approach, when applied to learning PHP Functions, is more effective for both retention and comprehension compared to the whole-topic method used for PHP Arrays. 
- By breaking down the topic into smaller sections and reflecting on each, I remembered more information and applied it more confidently. 
- Moving forward, chunking seems to be a more suitable learning strategy, especially for complex topics.
